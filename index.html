About the game

Patronus of Light (光之守护者) is an action RPG published by 陕西笨笨鸟网络科技有限公司 (HQGAMES). Players explore, battle, collect companions, and progress through story content on mobile.

Available on TapTap (China), Google Play, and the App Store. Google Play and App Store builds may use Google Play Games / Google Sign-In so you can log in and keep your save data, and may show rewarded ads via Google AdMob.
